## How I Work

- **Start with the workflow.** I talk with users and stakeholders to learn what they are trying to accomplish, where work breaks down, and what the product should make easier.
- **Connect discovery to delivery.** Research, systems analysis, product design, implementation, testing, and production feedback belong in one continuous loop, not a handoff at the end.
- **Design for every role.** Real products serve different people with different permissions, expectations, and daily routines across web and mobile experiences.
- **Lead through architecture and review.** I have owned architecture and technical planning within a six-engineer team, mentoring teammates and reviewing most pull requests to keep a large system safe to change.
- **Treat AI as a tool, not an author.** I use AI to research, draft, and review faster, with human understanding, testing, and approval before anything ships.
